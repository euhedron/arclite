A lexicon: the project's own vocabulary made explicit — its names and terms, with their canonical forms and meanings — so language drift is caught rather than hand-policed, and judgment can consult the project's vocabulary as declared grounds. Scope and shape are deliberately open, re-opened 2026-08-25: the earlier recorded shape (a `{term, casing}` JSON pack of org spellings, checked for casing drift) was a session's speculation adopted from a README idea (2026-08-15) and never operator-settled — it names one shallow, deterministic use, not the concept. Open: whether the lexicon is a spelling/casing check, a glossary of meanings runs consult as context, or both; how it classes against modular-specification's facets (a lexicon leans structured mapping where facets are free-form — plausibly its own kind under the classes-by-role doctrine); and what the sibling context engines' term/entity substrate (commissure, crux) contributes as the working exemplar. What stands either way: it is not a ruleset — names and meanings are grounds a judgment consults, not general principles.
