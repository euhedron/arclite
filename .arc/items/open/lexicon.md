A lexicon: the project's own vocabulary made explicit — its names and terms, with their canonical forms and meanings — so language drift is caught rather than hand-policed, and judgment can consult the project's vocabulary as declared grounds. Essentially a glossary: definitions and descriptions of core concepts and terms — the language and understandings meant to be consistent throughout the project. Like facets but one level up: high-level and implementation-independent — where facets describe the project, the lexicon establishes the meaning of the words the describing (and all judgment over the project) is conducted in — background context ensuring reasoning stands on a common language, with the further aim that the vocabulary used in content and code stays restricted and consistent. The operator's experiments live in `.arc.local`. Feeding the remaining shape: how it classes against modular-specification's facets (plausibly its own kind under the classes-by-role doctrine), and what the sibling context engines' term/entity substrate (commissure, crux) contributes as the working exemplar. What stands either way: it is not a ruleset — names and meanings are grounds a judgment consults, not general principles.
