A lexicon — essentially a glossary: the project's core concepts and terms with their canonical forms, definitions, and descriptions, so language drift is caught rather than hand-policed and judgment consults the project's vocabulary as declared grounds. Like facets but one level up: high-level and implementation-independent — where facets describe the project, the lexicon establishes the meaning of the words the describing (and all judgment over the project) is conducted in — background context ensuring reasoning stands on a common language and that the vocabulary used in content and code stays restricted and consistent. Feeding the remaining shape: how it classes against modular-specification's facets (plausibly its own kind under the classes-by-role doctrine), and what the sibling context engines' term/entity substrate (commissure, crux) contributes as the working exemplar. It is not a ruleset — names and meanings are grounds a judgment consults, not general principles.
