The repo lens extends to the remaining cwd-scoped views. The usage view embodies the principle — the launch cwd is a default lens, never a boundary — but items, rules, config, and feedback still resolve only the launch repo: the operator hit the boundary in items: another project's agenda unreachable from the cockpit. The instrument is the type-to-filter repo picker tui-usage-navigation reshapes selection into, applied per view: items browsing a selected repo's agenda, rules resolving a selected repo's ruleset and firing record, config a selected repo's layers, feedback a selected repo's inbox. One lens concept across the cockpit rather than per-view variants.
