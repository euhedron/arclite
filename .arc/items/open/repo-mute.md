Per-repo mute: disable a retired repo across arc's surfaces — the ledger lenses, status, and log, CLI and TUI alike — so ended experiments stop appearing without deleting their history. A muted repo stays in the ledger (the record is permanent); mute is a view-level default, disclosed wherever it filters. Setter and storage: a `muted_repos` settings key on the `disabled_rules` pattern — `arc config set muted_repos <paths>`, toggleable in the TUI's repo lenses — user-layer only, since a mute is the operator's lens over the operator's ledger (the mechanics-are-private doctrine), never a repo's tracked claim about itself.
