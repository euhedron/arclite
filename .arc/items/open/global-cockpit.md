`arc tui` launched anywhere — the home folder included — is the device-wide cockpit, not a mistake: the masthead's home-folder warning stays right for runs (whose context would scan the tree), but the cockpit reads discovery and the ledger, so the global launch is the point — the launch-cwd-as-default-lens principle completed. The home view: every arc-enabled project arc has discovered as rows, where *arc-enabled* means the repo has an `.arc/` directory (arc is set up there) — "discovered" is the discovery map's reach (repos seen in an agent session store or the ledger), not a full-disk scan for `.arc/` dirs, so the promise is scoped to what the map knows, never "every `.arc/` on the device." The map also holds discovered repos *without* `.arc/`; those appear as init candidates — not full cockpit rows, but offered for scaffolding in place (tui-init's entry), so the home view both oversees arc-enabled repos and onboards the rest. The rows join device-awareness's map with the ledger, showing per repo the rules count, items open and resolved, whether it is gated and which hooks are enabled, spend total and today, recency of the last audit, commits since the last passing gate round, and rounds per convergence sequence (gate-round-linkage's recorded unit — rounds and their outcomes, never an assertion that the push itself completed, which is the remote's truth); selecting a row opens the repo's detail — the evolution over time of commits versus audit rounds versus blocked/passed outcomes, with averages (rounds per convergence sequence, time per gate). Cross-project alerting surfaces here: agenda drift in any repo, thresholded measures through repo-drift-metrics' facility (context-window share, item counts at zero or extreme, disabled-rule counts, dirty-file counts), and recent-activity hotspots from the discovery map (recency is an mtime heuristic and stays labeled as such — a genuine waiting-on-the-operator signal needs a positive source, arc's own run markers or the agent's hooks, live-transcript-auditing's territory). The aggregate read the view exists for: the degree to which arc is impacting development across every repo — the stability and rate of change of rules, items, and order, and the loop's economics from the local ledger's history (spend per resolved item, per gate round, and their trends — whether the loop's compounding output pays for its spend); the economics read this machine's ledger, with CI spend joining the same rows once github-check-gate's ingest and committed project identity land (that staged handoff is the dependency, named). Consumes device-awareness, gate-round-linkage, repo-drift-metrics, and context-window-utilization; what is new here is the assembled home surface and its alert wiring.
