Distribution channels beyond the install-script and Homebrew channels, with crates.io owned by release-verification — independent deliverables, none blocking the others, each completing on its own: **cargo-binstall** (metadata in `Cargo.toml` so `cargo binstall arclite` pulls the prebuilt release binary rather than compiling), **Scoop** (a Scoop manifest) and **WinGet** (a WinGet package) — separate Windows channels that complete and verify independently, grouped here only as kin to the Homebrew tap, both auto-bumped by the release workflow the way the tap already is — and **signed releases** — an out-of-band signing key or GitHub build attestations, the real tamper-resistance the same-channel `SHA256SUMS` can't provide (it shares the release's trust root rather than standing outside it). Each lands with its own `verify-release.yml` check exercising it on a fresh runner, the way every existing channel is verified, and each completes independently — no internal order. Split from release-verification, which keeps the single crates.io deliverable.
