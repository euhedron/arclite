Distribution channels beyond the landed install-script + Homebrew + crates.io set, each its own deliverable, none blocking the others: **cargo-binstall** (metadata in `Cargo.toml` so `cargo binstall arclite` pulls the prebuilt release binary rather than compiling), **Scoop and WinGet** (a Scoop manifest and a WinGet package, the Windows analogues of the Homebrew tap, auto-bumped by the release workflow the way the tap already is), and **signed releases** — an out-of-band signing key or GitHub build attestations, the real tamper-resistance the same-channel `SHA256SUMS` can't provide (it shares the release's trust root rather than standing outside it). Each lands with its own `verify-release.yml` check exercising it on a fresh runner, the way every existing channel is verified. Ordering among the three is by demand, not dependency: whichever a confronted need pulls first. Split from release-verification, which keeps the single crates.io deliverable.
