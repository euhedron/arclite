Distribution channels beyond the install-script and Homebrew channels, with crates.io owned by release-verification — each its own deliverable, none blocking the others: **cargo-binstall** (metadata in `Cargo.toml` so `cargo binstall arclite` pulls the prebuilt release binary rather than compiling), **Scoop and WinGet** (a Scoop manifest and a WinGet package, the Windows analogues of the Homebrew tap, auto-bumped by the release workflow the way the tap already is), and **signed releases** — an out-of-band signing key or GitHub build attestations, the real tamper-resistance the same-channel `SHA256SUMS` can't provide (it shares the release's trust root rather than standing outside it). Each lands with its own `verify-release.yml` check exercising it on a fresh runner, the way every existing channel is verified. The three are independent, so no internal order is imposed: a completed channel is struck through in this item with its verify-check, the item resolves only when the last of the three completes, and if one becomes urgent before the others it splits into its own item then — the agenda's ordinary add/split — rather than forcing a sequence now.
