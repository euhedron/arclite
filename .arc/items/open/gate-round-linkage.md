Link the gate's runs into their rounds, and the rounds into their convergence story. Vocabulary pinned to the README's: a *round* is one push attempt (one hook invocation); a *run* is one gate command within it. The hook exports a round id — one env var, generated once per invocation — that every run in that invocation records; consecutive rounds on the same ref, ending in the one that passes, are the convergence sequence, derived in log/usage by adjacency over the recorded ids rather than stamped. Needs only the hook stamp and a record field.
