Link the gate's runs into their rounds, and the rounds into their convergence story. Vocabulary pinned to the README's: a *round* is one push attempt (one hook invocation); a *run* is one gate command within it. The hook exports a round id — one env var, generated once per invocation — that every run in that invocation records; consecutive rounds on the same ref, ending in the one that passes, are the convergence sequence, derived in log/usage by adjacency over the recorded ids rather than stamped. Needs only the gate-invocation stamp and a record field — the stamp's home today is the hook script, and it relocates into `arc gate` when gate-configuration later collapses the hook to a shim (a round is one gate invocation, wherever the gate runs from; the id survives the relocation unchanged).
