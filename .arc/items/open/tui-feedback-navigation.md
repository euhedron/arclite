The feedback view's entries become navigable, not just scrollable — the reshaping the log view already has, applied to the surface the operator caught scrolling blind. Today `FeedbackView` carries only a scroll `offset`: the combined inbox + outbound listing windows past the viewport, but no entry can be selected or opened, so a report or note truncated to one line in the list has no way to its full text from the cockpit. It gains a `selected` cursor over the two queues — `↑↓` moving the selection (scrolling to keep it in view) rather than scrolling the whole pane, `enter` opening the highlighted entry (an inbox note or an outbound report) to its full body, `esc` back to the list. Selection is also the precondition for acting on an entry in place as the closed-loop cockpit grows — triaging a note, refiling a report — the way the log view's selection is the seat for opening a run. The selection model is the log view's — a cursor moved by `↑↓`, `enter` to open — shared with tui-status-navigation; tui-usage-navigation is a sibling in reshaping TUI navigation but by a different mechanic (a type-to-filter repo picker, where `↑↓` stays scroll), not this cursor model.
