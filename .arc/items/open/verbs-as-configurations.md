Finish making a verb a configuration: its role directive, taxonomy, item schema, and gate field shipped as built-in defaults and overridable through settings like everything else. Today only the taxonomy is settings-configurable; the role lives as a data string on the Rust registry row (data, not phrasing hidden in code, but not yet settings-overridable), and the item schema and gate field are still compiled in. The role-directive-to-Markdown step — and its prompt-quality meta-ruleset — is owned by dedicated-prompts, which precedes this item; here, in order: the item schema, then the gate field, as settings data beside the taxonomy. "A new verb needn't mean a rebuild" is the end state's aim, not a claim it holds yet — and its own precondition is that verb *membership* stop being compiled: the CLI parse, the registry, the policy map, and settings' verb-name validation all enumerate verbs at compile time today, so a genuinely rebuild-free new verb needs a registered-verbs source those surfaces read, which this item's configurability work is the groundwork for, not the whole of.
