The rulespace registry — deliberately one coarse item, because it is a pointer: the registry (accounts, organizations, publishing and sharing, aggregated firing data — curation pressure compounding across users and orgs instead of one machine) is its own Euhedron product and repo, and its decomposition belongs to that repo's agenda, not arclite's. What is arclite's, as the registry lands: the arc-side integrations — euhedron login beside the provider API keys, the feedback channel's outbound transport (the captured `~/.arc/feedback.jsonl` queue is its client-side buffer), and the generalized arc-in-Actions offering (registry-owned per the github-check-gate boundary). Its open design questions — rule provenance/lineage across versions, trust and moderation of shared rules — travel with it.
