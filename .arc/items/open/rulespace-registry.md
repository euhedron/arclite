The rulespace registry — deliberately one coarse item, because it is a pointer: the registry (accounts, organizations, publishing and sharing, aggregated firing data — curation pressure compounding across users and orgs instead of one machine; the aggregation and arc-in-Actions boundaries with public-run-record and the github items are drawn on those items) is its own Euhedron product and repo, and its decomposition belongs to that repo's agenda, not arclite's. The direction of conformance is one-way: how the registry works is designed in its own seat — a dedicated design discussion with the operator precedes any build — and arclite, the volatile experimental side, adapts its rules, structure, and organization to the registry's infrastructure and vision once the registry's initial base form is live end to end; nothing here speculatively bends the registry toward arclite's current shape. What arclite carries meanwhile: rules live where they are used — the repo remains the source of truth for the rules it owns (the pack principle: audited where they live), the registry distribution and aggregation over that, publishing a content-hash projection with lineage so repo-versus-published drift is detectable signal, and consuming a matter of new ruleset source forms — a pinned git/GitHub ref (`github:owner/repo/path@rev`, zero-infrastructure, standing on existing git auth) and a registry ref (`rulespace:owner/pack@hash`) — composing with local sources like any other. The core local loop never requires the registry (fully-local operation with registry-and-local composition is the field norm — semgrep, verified 2026-08-25: <https://docs.semgrep.dev/running-rules>): the registry earns adoption on discovery, aggregated firing data, and trust, not on being the only pipe. arclite's own work as the registry lands: euhedron login beside the provider API keys, the feedback channel's outbound transport (the captured `~/.arc/feedback.jsonl` queue is its client-side buffer), the remote source forms above, and the App's hosted endpoint (the arc-side App design is github-app's item). Open questions — rule provenance/lineage across versions and forks, trust and moderation of shared rules — travel with the registry.
