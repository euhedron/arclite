Existing state, data, or structure gets no vote merely for existing. The default posture runs backward — decide the ideal shape first, then let old material argue its way in on demonstrated need — yet the reflex is to accommodate what's there: compatibility kept "just in case", migrations bent around artifacts nobody asked to keep, designs contorted to preserve a shape whose only defense is presence. The imagined beneficiary of that accommodation is usually speculative; the cost to the ideal shape is real and immediate. Flag work shaped to preserve or accommodate prior state where no confronted need — a user, a dependency, a stated requirement — demanded it.
