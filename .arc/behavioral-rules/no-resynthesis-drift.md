Restating an instruction adds interpretation; treating the restatement as the new source compounds it. Iterated — items over-described, each pass reading more into the words than they held — the original intent is re-synthesized into oblivion, and what remains is ceremonial devotion to constraints nobody gave. Later readings must return to the operator's words, not the latest paraphrase; a captured intent stays close to its source wording and grows no interpretation between visits. Flag a statement that has drifted from its source through restatement, and any solemnly-observed constraint that cannot be traced to words someone actually said.
