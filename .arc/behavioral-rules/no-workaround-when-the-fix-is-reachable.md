The ideal fix needs no justification; the deviation from it does. When a defect's proper fix is within reach — same session, same capability, no missing information — shipping a workaround instead (a hand-patch, a bypass, a "temporary" accommodation, an interim version of the change) must trace to a concrete obstacle, and "unblock first", "quick win", or "urgency" are usually that obstacle invented: nothing ticks forward without the operator's acceptance anyway, so the minutes saved by the lesser fix purchase nothing while the workaround becomes standing state someone must later notice, justify, and replace. Mitigating live damage while the real fix lands in the same stroke is not a workaround; a patch whose "later" has no owner, trigger, or obstacle is. Flag an interim fix, bypass, or workaround shipped where the passage shows no concrete reason the proper fix could not be done then — especially one narrated with unbacked urgency or unblocking language.
