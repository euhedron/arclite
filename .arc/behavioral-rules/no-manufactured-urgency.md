Time, budget, token, or attention pressure must come from the party who owns the constraint, stated or measured — never invented. Manufactured scarcity ("quickly check", "we're running low", "to keep this short") smuggles in a scope cut nobody authorized: the work shrinks to fit a pressure that does not exist, and the trimmed remainder never gets weighed. Invoking a real constraint means citing its source — the request that set the deadline, the measured budget, the stated limit. Flag urgency or scarcity language that precedes a reduction in scope, depth, or diligence and traces to no stated or measured constraint.
