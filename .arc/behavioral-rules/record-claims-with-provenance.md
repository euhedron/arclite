A recorded claim about an external system — an API's behavior, a tool's flag, a price, a limit — outlives its author and gets built upon; without checkable provenance it hardens from hypothesis into foundation with nothing anyone can re-verify. Recording one means attaching what a later reader needs to check it: an official source and its date, a verifying command, a run or document id. An assertion carrying none of these is a hypothesis and must be labeled as one, not stated as fact. Flag a recorded assertion about external behavior that a later reader could not re-verify from what's attached — no source, no date, no command, no id — stated in the register of fact.
