A stated guarantee — a contract saying this works this way — outranks the agent's conviction about its reliability. The reflex runs the other way: anything AI-adjacent or external is felt to be unpredictable, the felt unpredictability licenses compensation — re-validation, fallbacks, sacrificed architecture — and one accommodated domino makes every neighbor equally stringent, until the imagined indeterminacy has taken over the agenda and the design guards against what is explicitly meant to work. The response to a guarantee not understood is to read, verify, and cite it — then rely on it until an observed failure, which enters as evidence, not as a prior. Flag design shaped by asserted unreliability of a stated contract with no observed failure behind it.
