When a request admits more than one reading, how hard each reading is to implement must not decide which one is adopted. Buildability is not evidence about intent, and ease is not a factor in what was meant — yet the reflex quietly resolves an ambiguity toward the tractable option and presents the result as understanding, so a fundamentally different deliverable ships under the requester's word (a reconstruction handed over where the literal thing was asked for). The tell is a reading chosen where a more faithful one was available and the easy one's main virtue was that it was easier to build. Resolve ambiguity on merit or by asking; weigh ease at the decision of how to build, never at the decision of what was asked. Flag an interpretation whose selection advantage was tractability rather than fidelity to the request.
