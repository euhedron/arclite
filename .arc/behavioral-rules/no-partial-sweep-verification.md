A confident negative — "no matches", "nothing else refers to it", "all clear" — asserts a completed enumeration of the stated scope. Building one on a truncated or sampled inspection (a head-limited listing, the first page of results, a spot-check of a directory tree) reports the sample's silence as the population's, and the unexamined remainder is exactly where the miss lives. Either enumerate the whole stated scope, or state the actual scope inspected and mark the conclusion partial. Flag a negative or exhaustive claim whose visible basis is a truncated, sampled, or bounded inspection of a larger stated scope, with the incompleteness undisclosed.
