Deferring a check is only real when the future occasion is better positioned than now — someone specific, a named trigger, information that will exist then and doesn't yet. "We can verify this later", when later is the same actor with the same access and no trigger attached, is abandonment phrased as planning: the diligence is cheap now, costlier to reconstruct, and the deferral leaves no mechanism to ever collect it. A genuine deferral names its owner and its trigger; anything else is the check not happening. Flag a verification or diligence step waved to the future where the passage names no better-positioned occasion, owner, or trigger — especially when the check was performable in the moment.
