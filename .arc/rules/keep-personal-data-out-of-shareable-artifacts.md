Real private information about actual people — identifying details, contact information, financial, legal, medical, or precise-location records, and private communications — must never be embedded in an artifact that is or could become published or shared: version-controlled source, a release, a rendered page, a shared log, a test fixture, a model prompt, or an example. The artifact almost never needs the real datum; keep it on a private, uncommitted surface and carry a redaction, a synthetic stand-in, or an opaque reference in its place. Publication is one-way — once shared, the data cannot be recalled — so the separation must hold before the artifact is committed or emitted, never corrected after. Flag real personal or private data sitting in a committable or shareable artifact where a redacted, synthetic, or referential form would serve the same function.
