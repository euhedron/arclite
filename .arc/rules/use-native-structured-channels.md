When a model's output will be parsed or acted on downstream, request it through the provider's native structured channel — schema-constrained output or strict tool calls, where conformance is enforced by constrained decoding rather than asked for in the prompt — and switch the enforcement on where it is opt-in (strict modes). Free-text contracts ("return only JSON…"), fenced-block scraping, and regex extraction are unenforced hope: they fail unpredictably and push a validation burden onto every consumer. Reserve prose output for prose deliverables. Flag an integration that parses model output arriving over an unenforced channel when the platform offers a native structured one, and a structured channel used with its enforcement left off.
